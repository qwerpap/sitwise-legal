Privacy Policy

Sitwise: Senior Chair Workout · Last updated September 28, 2026

Sitwise offers gentle seated exercise sessions with pictures and an on-device voice guide. It works on your iPhone. There are no accounts and no server that stores your activity, and we don't sell or share your data.

What stays on your device

  • Your answers from the first-run questions (how moving feels, your goal, stiff areas, preferred length and time of day).
  • The sessions you finish: date, which session and how long.
  • Comfort settings: voice, speech speed, text size, contrast and reminder time.

This information is stored only in Sitwise's own storage on your iPhone. It is never uploaded, never used for advertising and never shared.

Voice guide

Spoken instructions are produced by your iPhone's built-in speech voices. No audio is recorded and nothing is sent anywhere. Sitwise does not use the microphone or camera.

Family summary

Sitwise can build a one-page PDF of the sessions you did. It is made on your iPhone and leaves it only if you share it yourself.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the one-time purchase are processed by Apple through the App Store. Sitwise receives only the status of your purchase, never your payment details. We use a subscription-management service (Adapty) that receives an anonymous app-install identifier, your purchase status and which onboarding screen you reached, to validate purchases and measure how the purchase screen performs. It never receives your answers or your activity.

Notifications

If you allow them, Sitwise schedules a daily local reminder on your iPhone. No push server is involved.

Analytics and tracking

Sitwise does not track you across apps or websites, does not use advertising identifiers and contains no third-party analytics beyond the subscription service above.

Not medical advice

Sitwise is for general wellness. It does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition. Check with your doctor before starting a new exercise routine, and stop if anything hurts.

Deleting your data

Remove any finished session with its cross in My week, or delete Sitwise from your iPhone to remove everything it stored.

Children

Sitwise is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
