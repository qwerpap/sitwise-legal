Common questions

  • The voice is silent. Check that the ringer volume is up and that Comfort › Read each move aloud is on. To add a clearer voice: Settings › Accessibility › Spoken Content › Voices.
  • Text is too small. Turn on Comfort › Larger text, or raise the size in Settings › Display & Brightness › Text Size.
  • Restore a purchase. Comfort › Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID.
  • Cancel a subscription. iPhone Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Sitwise.
